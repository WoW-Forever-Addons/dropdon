# Changes

## 0.13.0

- **More loot:** loot lists of instances were extended with data from the latest sources, nothing was removed. Blackmaw Hold now has its 7 bosses with loot, The Shaper's Terrace its bosses with loot, and Blackrock Depths, Stratholme, Dire Maul and Scholomance have more drops. Item quality colours are filled in too.
- **Tabs and list headers in the game's look:** group headers in lists are the game's own bars, with plus and minus on the right.
- **Options open in their own window:** the options button, the cog, the minimap button and `/dd options` open only Dropdon's own options window; the game's AddOns list has just one button leading there. Checkmarks have a light border, and long tooltips sit beside the mouse.

## 0.12.1

- **One option for the window style:** the switch "Windows in the game's look" is now the choice "Window style" with "Don look" and "Blizzard look", named the same in every Don addon. Your setting stays as it was.
- **Shared Don library:** updated to the current version of the Don addons.

## 0.12.0

- **Two new dungeons:** Blackmaw Hold (levels 55-60) and The Shaper's Terrace (58-60) with their quest rewards; their bosses and loot follow as soon as they are known.
- **Full names in the large window:** instance and boss names such as "Ruins of Lordaeron" or "Shadowfang Keep" now fit, a wider window gives the names more room first.
- **Selected row easy to read:** in the game's look the level of the selected instance stands in white on the gold band.
- **Small window:** in the game's look the title is the instance's name, e.g. "Ruins of Lordaeron".

## 0.11.0

- **Dropdon in the game's look:** the large loot window now has the game's own frame, buttons and lists, and the small window sits on the game's tooltip ground. The options open in the game's settings (Options > AddOns > Dropdon). This is the new default; the Don look is one switch away under Appearance, "Windows in the game's look".
- **`/dd diag`:** new report for bug reports. It opens in a window you can copy from; `/dd diag chat` prints it in the chat.
- **Level colours like the game's:** the level range of each instance is coloured the way the game colours quest levels for your level.

## 0.10.0

- **New dungeon: Alcaz Prison** (levels 48-53) with its four bosses, their loot and the quest rewards.
- **More loot in Zul'Farrak:** the new Forever items of several bosses, and a few new quest rewards elsewhere.

## 0.9.0

- **Two new dungeons:** The Drowned City (5 bosses) and Krol'dok Stronghold (6 bosses) with their loot and quest rewards; a few new items in Gnomeregan, Razorfen Downs, Zul'Farrak, the Sunken Temple and the Scarlet Monastery.
- **Tooltips at the mouse:** item tooltips of the wide rows open at the cursor instead of beside the whole window.
- **No more "Retrieving item information":** while the server has not answered for an item, Dropdon shows its name, kind, item level and required level from its own list.
- **Window keeps its top edge:** after moving it, the window grows downwards instead of from its middle.

## 0.8.1

- **Item names without the server:** items the WoW Forever server does not answer for (new and changed ones) show their name from Dropdon's own list instead of staying empty.

## 0.8.0

- **Minimap button:** golden ring with Dropdon's logo; right-click opens a menu with the loot window, the mini window, four switches, "All options" and "Hide minimap button".
- **Classic Era:** items of WoW Forever that the Era client does not know are hidden and not counted (no more "Loading item...").
- **Boss counter** reads "4 / 6".
- **Addon list:** the addon has its own logo in the game's addon list.

## 0.7.0

- **First release on CurseForge.**
- **Loot data from the current WoW Forever files of All The Things:** 31 instances. New: Scholomance, Zul'Gurub and The Drowned City (only its quest so far). Items of other game versions (Season of Discovery) are gone from Blackrock Spire, Stratholme, the raids and other instances.
- The new Forever dungeons (Krol'Dok Stronghold, Alcaz Island Prison, Blackmaw Hold, Shaper's Terrace) follow as soon as their loot is known.

## 0.6.0

- **New look:** both windows in the Don style of all Don addons. The large window has three columns (instances, bosses, loot), can be dragged from 720x420 to 1300x900 and keeps its size. Under 800 px wide the instance is chosen from a list above the bosses.
- **Instances:** search box, Dungeons and Raids fold away, the level range in the game's quest colours, a blue mark where you are.
- **Bosses:** the title in brackets as a second line, "fitting / all" items on the right, wings as headings.
- **Loot as a table:** item level and required level (red when above yours), sortable by name, item level and required level, shift-click links an item. The chip "Hidden" shows what your filters hide, dimmed with a red symbol and the reason in the tooltip.
- **Small window:** a switch "Only for me", the loot of a boss as a grid of item symbols, the width can be dragged (220 to 400).

## 0.5.0

- **Options window:** Esc > Options > AddOns > Dropdon or `/dd options`: lock, size, background opacity, dimming in combat and reset position for both windows; the minimap button, choosing the instance you walk into and the tooltip line.
- **Your language:** all texts in French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Korean and Traditional Chinese besides English and German. Boss and instance names come in your game's language once you have seen them in the game (target, mouse over or nameplate, entering the instance).
- **Cleaner lists:** a divider before Shared drops and Quest rewards; only items that exist in WoW Forever (items of later expansions are gone).
