# Changes

## 0.8.1

- **Item names without the server:** items the WoW Forever server does not answer for (new and changed ones) show their name from Dropdon's own list instead of staying empty.

## 0.8.0

- **Minimap button:** golden ring with Dropdon's logo; right-click opens a menu with the loot window, the mini window, four switches, "All options" and "Hide minimap button".
- **Classic Era:** items of WoW Forever that the Era client does not know are hidden and not counted (no more "Loading item...").
- **Boss counter** reads "4 / 6".
- **Addon list:** the addon has its own logo in the game's addon list.

## 0.7.0

- **First release on CurseForge.**
- **Loot data from the current WoW Forever files of All The Things:** 31 instances. New: Scholomance, Zul'Gurub and The Drowned City (only its quest so far). Items of other game versions (Season of Discovery) are gone from Blackrock Spire, Stratholme, the raids and other instances.
- The new Forever dungeons (Krol'Dok Stronghold, Alcaz Island Prison, Blackmaw Hold, Shaper's Terrace) follow as soon as their loot is known.

## 0.6.0

- **New look:** both windows in the Don style of all Don addons. The large window has three columns (instances, bosses, loot), can be dragged from 720x420 to 1300x900 and keeps its size. Under 800 px wide the instance is chosen from a list above the bosses.
- **Instances:** search box, Dungeons and Raids fold away, the level range in the game's quest colours, a blue mark where you are.
- **Bosses:** the title in brackets as a second line, "fitting / all" items on the right, wings as headings.
- **Loot as a table:** item level and required level (red when above yours), sortable by name, item level and required level, shift-click links an item. The chip "Hidden" shows what your filters hide, dimmed with a red symbol and the reason in the tooltip.
- **Small window:** a switch "Only for me", the loot of a boss as a grid of item symbols, the width can be dragged (220 to 400).

## 0.5.0

- **Options window:** Esc > Options > AddOns > Dropdon or `/dd options`: lock, size, background opacity, dimming in combat and reset position for both windows; the minimap button, choosing the instance you walk into and the tooltip line.
- **Your language:** all texts in French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Korean and Traditional Chinese besides English and German. Boss and instance names come in your game's language once you have seen them in the game (target, mouse over or nameplate, entering the instance).
- **Cleaner lists:** a divider before Shared drops and Quest rewards; only items that exist in WoW Forever (items of later expansions are gone).
